Questions & Answers

Frequently Asked

Everything you need to know about LUX products and services.

We use 18K and 24K gold, sterling silver (925), platinum, and ethically sourced gemstones including diamonds, sapphires, rubies, and emeralds. All materials are certified conflict-free.

Standard shipping takes 3–5 business days. Express shipping (1–2 days) is available at checkout. International orders typically arrive within 7–14 business days. All orders over $200 receive free express shipping.

Yes! We offer a 30-day hassle-free return and exchange policy. Items must be in their original, unworn condition with all original packaging. Custom engraved pieces are non-returnable.

Your security is our priority. We use 256-bit SSL encryption and are fully PCI-DSS compliant. We accept all major credit cards, PayPal, and Apple Pay. We never store your card details.

We recommend storing jewelry in the provided velvet pouch, away from moisture and direct sunlight. Clean gently with a soft, lint-free cloth. Avoid contact with perfumes, chlorine, and harsh chemicals. A full care guide is included with every purchase.
