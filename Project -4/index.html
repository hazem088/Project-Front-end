Frequently Asked

Everything you need to know about NOVA products and services.

We use 18K and 24K gold, sterling silver (925), platinum, and ethically sourced gemstones including diamonds, sapphires, rubies, and emeralds. All materials are certified conflict-free.
Standard shipping takes 3–5 business days. Express shipping (1–2 days) is available at checkout. International orders typically arrive within 7–14 business days. All orders over $200 receive free express shipping.
Yes! We offer a 30-day hassle-free return and exchange policy. Items must be in their original unworn condition with all packaging. Custom engraved pieces are non-returnable.
